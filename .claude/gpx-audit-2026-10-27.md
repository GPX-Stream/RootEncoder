# GPX override audit, 2026-10-27

Audit of every numbered GPX patch (R1–R53) and every `GPX patch` marker in `gpx-alpha1`
against `pedro/master` @ `f1bbda7ca`, plus a marker and comment consistency pass. Requested by
Andy the same day. Read-only reviewers on Sonnet 5.5 compared both sides, one group of items each.
This file records the result and the open calls; the per-merge record is R52 and R53 in
`.claude/gpx-reapply-plan-2.8.0.md`.

## Result in one line

No numbered patch retires. Upstream has no equivalent for any of them; the three it overlaps were
already settled in R52 (R14's DTLS client role, R43, R44).
Of the eight unnumbered `GPX patch` sites, seven keep their code and one marker is wrong (the code is
upstream's). Four of the eight carry a wrong or incomplete marker.

## Marker and comment consistency

Checked all 53 code files that differ from upstream.

- Every changed region now has a marker within reach except `StreamBase.kt` (nine regions) and two new
  test files. The policy already calls `StreamBase.kt` a dense grouped rewrite whose markers are an
  index. Eleven missing markers on certain attributions were added (comments only).
- Forms in use: `GPX R<n>` (217), `GPX R<n>b` (5: `R3b`, `R16b`), `GPX patch` (6), and
  `GPX fork change 8/9/10` (14). The policy allows only the first and third.
- `R34` has markers in `GlStreamInterface.kt` but no checklist entry in the plan.
- No file carries a corrupted character; the one seen in a terminal was a display artifact.

## Open calls for Andy

1. **Push `gpx-alpha1`** (R53 filter + the marker commit; two commits on top of the pushed head).
   QA by commit needs it. Nothing else is pending push.
2. **R29: keep 3000 ms?** Upstream uses 1000 ms. A short bound can cut a healthy but slow flush on
   a card; nothing shows 1000 ms ever loses data on a healthy card, and nothing shows it doesn't.
3. **R7 and upstream's optional silence check.** Both are in the tree; upstream's stays off. Keep
   both (current), or drop upstream's so there is one mechanism.
4. **R4: stream-start keyframe.** Upstream asks for one only when a recording is already running.
   Keep ours (current).
5. **Marker naming.** Give `fork change 8/9/10` and `R3b` / `R16b` proper numbers, or allow those
   forms in the policy.
6. **`StreamBase.kt` markers.** Leave as the policy has it, or mark the nine regions. One of them
   (the audio-codec twin of `setVideoCodec`, near line 1370) has no item number.
7. **R34** has no plan entry. Write one (documents only).
8. **A live WHIP publish to Millicast.** Upstream's per-stream `msid` lines now sit beside our
   `msid-semantic: WMS` form. Only a real publish shows whether the ingest accepts it. Also the
   SRT retransmit under loss.
9. **Reviewer-raised, not new in the merge, optional:** the ICE retransmit cannot work with
   `SocketType.JAVA` (the app uses the default `KTOR`); a disconnect during connect can report a
   false "Error configure stream"; the early-return paths in the connect job do not close the socket;
   the DTLS wait is now 10 s, so a failed DTLS reports up to 5 s later; `candidateResponses` is now
   written and never read.
10. **`gpxstream-app` follow-through:** `CLAUDE.md` and `docs/runbooks/rootencoder-fork.md` still
    name `gpx-2.8`; for QA the pin becomes the full commit SHA of `gpx-alpha1`'s head.
11. **Worktree** `RootEncoder/.claude/worktrees/alpha1` stays until QA is done (removal will hit the
    Windows long-path error).
12. **Wrong or incomplete markers on the unnumbered sites** (table above): retag the two
    `BaseEncoder.java` sites and `SrtClient.kt:282` to their item numbers, restore upstream's wording on
    the BT.709 block, give `sourcesRunning` a number or a fuller header, and trim two comments that
    narrate history or quote upstream. Comments only.
13. **`SrtClient.kt:282` overwrites `socketTimeout` on every connect.** A host that sets the public
    `socketTimeout` has its value replaced. No caller in this repo sets it. Keep as is, or only
    derive it when the host has not set one.
